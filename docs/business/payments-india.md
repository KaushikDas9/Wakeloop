# Payments from India (if you decide to charge)

Stripe payments are invite-only in India, but these work. Check each provider's live pricing and terms.

| Provider | India payouts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Polar** | Yes (payouts via Stripe Connect; India is on its supported list) | Merchant of record; about 4% + 40c per a comparison site; needs a normal Indian bank account (Wise/Payoneer/Revolut-style accounts usually fail verification) |
| **Dodo Payments** | India-based company | Merchant of record; 4% + $0.40, +1.5% international, 4% + 15c for Indian cards/UPI per its own pages; digital goods only; confirm individual onboarding |
| **Paddle** | India is a supported country | Merchant of record; about 5% + 50c per a comparison site; check its seller onboarding requirements |
| **Gumroad** | Local INR bank payouts | Flat 10% fee; bank payouts take 2-7 business days |
| **Lemon Squeezy** | Listed, but Indian merchants report restrictions since the Stripe acquisition | Skip for a new account |
| **Razorpay** | Yes | Gateway, not a merchant of record: you handle GST and foreign tax yourself |

Also check: GST and income-tax treatment of foreign income, and any bank/FEMA documentation. Ask a chartered accountant; this is not tax advice.

## Pricing stance

Start free and open source. Competitors price a paid app at $10-19 one-time, but free apps (Belay, Adrafinil) are already good. A sponsor/tip link (Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi, GitHub Sponsors) costs nothing. Add a paid tier only if a validated feature justifies it. Expect side income at best; typically only a small share of free users ever pay.
